# Numbers, units and time

> ✗ **Don't** — "three trunks" · "1.2GB" · "45ms" · "2:15pm" · "MOS 3.60000"
>
> ✓ **Do** — "3 trunks" · "1.2 GB" · "45 ms" · "14:15 AEST" · "MOS 3.6"

**Numerals always.** Use digits, including one to nine.

**Units.** A space between value and unit: 1.2 GB, 45 ms, 12 kbps. Percentages
close up: 99.95%.

**Precision.** Match precision to the decision. MOS to one decimal, packet loss
to two, counts whole. Do not pad with zeros.

**Time.** 24-hour clock with an explicit timezone. Relative under an hour,
absolute after.

**Ranges.** En dash, no spaces: 09:00–17:00.
