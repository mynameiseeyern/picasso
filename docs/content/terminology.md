# Terminology

> ✗ **Don't** — a screen that says "tenant" in the header, "customer" in the table and "org" in the filter.
>
> ✓ **Do** — one term, chosen once, used in all three.

**Pick one and hold it.** tenant, customer, organisation and site are not
interchangeable. Choose one per concept and use only that word.

**Explain our concepts.** Say what Clarion means by a probe, a threshold, a
fleet. These are ours, so they need defining.

**Never explain theirs.** Assume SIP, jitter, MOS, SBC and packet loss need no
gloss.
