# Errors

Two lines, in that order.

> ✗ **Don't** — "You entered an invalid query."
>
> ✓ **Do** — "No results match these filters. Clear the date range to widen the search."

**Line one is the fact.** What broke, and what it affects. Name the thing, not
the layer it failed in.

**Line two is the action.** What the reader can do now, or who can. If there is
nothing to do, say what happens next and when.

**Never blame the reader.** The filter returned nothing; the person did not
fail. Avoid "you entered", "invalid" and "illegal".

**No dead ends.** An error with no next step is an unfinished error.
